Privacy policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

What we store on your device

When you create a profile, SkillSphere saves your display name, chosen interests, skills, roadmaps, progress and an activity log in your browser's local storage. It also saves your cookie choice. This data never leaves your device unless you turn on cloud sync.

Optional cloud sync

If you allow optional storage and sign in, your email address, a password (handled by our authentication provider, Supabase) and a copy of your profile data are stored on Supabase servers so you can use them on other devices. The Supabase code library is loaded from the jsDelivr network only after you allow optional storage. Your sign-in session is kept in local storage.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, tracking pixels or analytics.
  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models.

Cookies and local storage

Essential storage keeps the app working: your profiles and your consent choice. Optional storage is used only for cloud sync sign-in. Use "Cookie settings" in the footer to change your choice at any time. Choosing "Essential only" signs you out of cloud sync.

Your choices

  • Export your data from the Profile page as a JSON file.
  • Delete a profile, or reset it, from the app. This removes it from this device and from cloud sync if you are signed in.
  • Clear your browser's site data to remove everything stored locally.
  • To delete your cloud account, contact us using the details below.

Children

SkillSphere is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when our practices change.
